About me

My background is in sociology, which gave me a natural curiosity about people and how they interact. For the last three years I've worked in CRM, mostly in the fashion industry, where I learned to pay close attention to what makes people respond, return and feel looked after.

A love of fashion and the arts means I care deeply about aesthetics and how things look and feel. UX is where all of this comes together, and it's become my real passion: understanding people, designing with care, and making the experience beautiful as well as useful.

I'm now an MSc student in London, especially interested in how people understand, trust and collaborate with AI, and in designing interfaces that make that feel natural.
